# What is MetalOps?

Welcome to MetalOps. I’m Adrian, and this is my space for exploring systems and infrastructure, from low-level software to the systems we operate in production.

MetalOps is an independent engineering practice and technical publication. The idea is simple: infrastructure should be understandable, measurable, recoverable, and under the operator’s control.

**Build. Measure. Break. Understand.**

I’m interested in building resilient systems, experimenting with self-hosting and bare metal, and understanding the tradeoffs behind the tools we choose. Sometimes the right answer is a familiar solution; sometimes an unconventional one earns its complexity.

MetalOps is not a news feed or a hype channel. I want the writing here to be deliberate, opinionated, and grounded in practical experience. Some articles will be technical deep dives; others will explore architecture decisions, system design tradeoffs, and operational lessons.

Over time, I plan to document:

- Architecture experiments and what they reveal
- Design patterns that have aged well
- Decisions I would not repeat
- Tools that earn their place

Welcome to MetalOps.
