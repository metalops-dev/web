## Platform

web

## Users

MetalOps serves infrastructure and systems engineers as well as technical learners. Readers may be building or operating infrastructure, studying low-level or distributed systems, or developing practical skills across these areas.

## Product Purpose

MetalOps is an independent engineering practice and personal technical publication, created by Adrian Villanueva. It shares technical writing, practical research and open-source work spanning bare metal to distributed systems.

Readers should gain practical understanding they can apply, access to reproducible investigations and findings, and discover useful projects and tools.

## Positioning

MetalOps brings together systems, infrastructure, distributed systems and data, reliability, and AI infrastructure under a shared engineering philosophy: infrastructure should be understandable, measurable, recoverable and under the operator's control.

## Operating Context

The existing site is an Astro-based technical publication with articles, tags, series, RSS, an about page and other resource pages. A proposed future structure covering writing, projects, experiments, engineering decisions, infrastructure, notes and reading is recorded in `metalops-identity-proposal.md`; it is a direction, not a description of already published work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing visual identity while reviewing and evolving the site's identity.
- Existing published articles and their URLs should remain accessible; the proposed structure is not yet implemented.
- Do not represent planned projects, experiments, infrastructure practices or organizational capacity as established facts.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: MetalOps / metalops.dev.
- Independent and personal: MetalOps sits between an individual publication and an independent engineering practice; Adrian is visibly associated with it.
- Working ethos: build, measure, break, understand.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing articles: `src/content/blog/`.
- Existing site and publication: `src/pages/`.
- Current proposed mission, pillars and future structure: `metalops-identity-proposal.md`.
- Existing bookmark categories in `src/data/bookmarks.yaml` have no entries; do not present them as a populated resource collection.

## Product Principles

1. Favor understanding and practical evidence over hype.
2. Make methods, tradeoffs and limitations clear when publishing engineering work.
3. Treat operability and recovery as part of infrastructure engineering.
4. Ground claims in actual published work and preserve access to existing material.
5. Serve experienced practitioners and learners without sacrificing technical substance.
